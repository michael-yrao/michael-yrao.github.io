How Prim's Algorithm Works

This game is a playable Minimum Spanning Tree builder. Prim's grows one connected tree outward from city 0, at every step attaching the cheapest edge from the tree to a city not yet in it — the exact shape as the learner's own minCostConnectPoints solution.

Scoring

Your total edge cost is compared against Prim's optimal cost for the same board. Tie it exactly for an Optimal! win — every optimal win adds to your best score, saved locally.
