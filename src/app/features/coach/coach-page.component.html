A spaced-repetition coach you talk to in Claude Code — not a spreadsheet you fill out by hand.

What it is

Instead of maintaining a tracker yourself, you talk to it the way you'd talk to a mentor sitting next to you: you say when you're starting a problem, ask it questions while you work, and tell it honestly how the attempt went. It handles the scheduling, the spaced repetition, and keeping you honest — your job is just to do the reps.

What it keeps honest

  • 🟢 Clean means coding, not recalling. A grade of Clean requires an actual blank-page attempt — no rounding up.
  • No spoilers. It answers clarifying questions freely, but won't hand you the approach unless you're truly stuck.
  • An effort budget. Your day is capped so the schedule protects you from burnout instead of piling on.
